# Registering domains with one.com

Get the voucher coupon from Teams. 

Remember that it's optional to use one.com and you are welcome to use other methods. 

Other services offer student discounts. 


---

# Steps I

Note that the design of the website may change over time.

1. Go to [one.com - Academia Partners](https://www.one.com/en-gb/about/academia-partners/)

2. Click the "Get started" button.

3. Search for the domain you want to register in the search field and click "Add domain".

4. In the right side in the checkout there is a drawer with the title "Do you have a voucher code?" Click on it to expand it and enter the voucher. The price should now be reduced to 0 DKK. Click "Continue". Do not add any additional services.

5. Create a new customer account as a "Private customer" and click "Continue". Fill in the required information and click "Continue". You must use your real email and phone number since you will get an activation link + code. 

6. For Punktum.dk's conditions, scroll down and select the checkbox "I want one.com to administrate my **** domains" and then click "Approve".

In your case it means that when you wish to terminate the domain you allow one.com to do it on your behalf rather than you having to contact another company to do it.

7. You must choose a payment method though it will not prompt you for card details.

8. You will receive two emails (an invoice and a confirmation email) and a text message. Click on the "Verify your order" link and type in the code you received in the text message. Click "Activate".

---

# Steps II

9. Take a break! It can take up to an hour before you get the domain. 

You will receive an email from Punktum.dk asking you to activate your new domain. You must complete this activation if you want to use the domain. If you do not activate the domain, the MX and TXT records will not propagate and will remain pending indefinitely.

10. In the confirmation email, click on "Create a password" and set a password for your account. Then log into the "Control Panel".

The plan should come with free email hosting, so feel free to set up an email address to experiment with it.

11. Later on, you will receive an email from Punktum.dk in order to "Gennemfør ID- og datakontrol". This is a legal requirement in Denmark [jf. Domænelovens §18](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2014/164). Click on "GÅ TIL ID-KONTROL" in the email and log into MitID. Click "Bekfræft og afslut ID-kontrol" and then click "Bekræft".
